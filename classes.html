Before You Arrive

What to Expect

First time at Barre & Sculpt? Here's everything you need to know to make the most of your experience.

Arrive 10 Min Early

Check in at the front desk, set up your space, and settle in before class begins.

Wear Grip Socks

Required for all classes. Available for purchase at the studio if you forget yours.

Bring Water

Stay hydrated! Bring a refillable bottle — we have filtered water stations throughout.

All Levels Welcome

Modifications offered for every exercise. Our instructors meet you exactly where you are.
