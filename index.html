About Our Studio

Where Elegance
Meets Strength

Founded in 2018, Barre & Sculpt is a boutique fitness studio dedicated to the art of movement. Our intimate setting ensures personalized attention in every class, with a maximum of 12 students per session.

We blend ballet-inspired techniques with modern fitness science to create workouts that are challenging yet accessible. Whether you're a seasoned dancer or trying barre for the first time, you'll feel welcomed and supported.
